# JD Fit Risk Rubric

Always create a risk warning when optimizing for a JD.

## Fit Levels

- **高匹配**: most hard requirements are supported by evidence; risks are mostly interview storytelling or metric defense.
- **中匹配**: role direction fits, but 1-2 important requirements are partial or weak.
- **勉强匹配**: multiple key requirements lack proof; resume can position adjacent evidence but interview risk is high.
- **不建议投递**: hard requirements are missing and cannot be credibly bridged before interview.

## Risk Types

Hard-gate risks:

- Degree, major, years of experience, city, language, certificate, work authorization, industry background.
- Tech stack, product domain, sales channel, data tooling, portfolio requirement.
- Missing portfolio/work sample for roles where proof is expected: design, product, development, content, data, campus projects.

Evidence risks:

- JD asks for a capability, but resume only has adjacent work.
- Candidate lists tools without project use.
- Candidate claims ownership but cannot explain decision process.
- Candidate's prior role is in a different function/industry and the transferable evidence is not explicit.

Credibility risks:

- Metrics lack baseline or denominator.
- Result seems too large for candidate's role.
- Project description hides team contribution.
- Terms imply seniority beyond actual experience.
- Resume shows unexplained gaps, overlapping dates, or a short full-time tenure pattern.
- Claimed level is higher than evidence: specialist wording for junior evidence, manager wording without team/budget/decision scope.

Career stability and transition risks:

- **Timeline gap**: >6 months after graduation or between jobs needs a clear, truthful explanation.
- **Short full-time tenure pattern**: multiple full-time roles under 12 months may raise stability concerns.
- **Role-span risk**: target role is far from prior work and transferable evidence is weak.
- **Industry migration risk**: industry-specific hard requirements are missing or only adjacent.

Lightweight tenure weighting:

| Employment type | <6 months | 6-12 months | >12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time | high risk unless explained | medium risk | usually low risk |
| Internship | usually low risk | low risk | not a concern |
| Project/contract | low if scoped | low if scoped | low if scoped |
| RPO/headhunting project | low if project-based | low if project-based | not a concern |

Interview exposure risks:

- "这个项目你个人贡献是什么？"
- "这个指标怎么算的？数据口径是什么？"
- "如果重新做一次，你会改哪里？"
- "你为什么适合这个岗位，而不是继续做原方向？"

## Output Format

Use this exact structure:

```text
匹配结论：
主要匹配点：
硬门槛风险：
能力证据风险：
项目可信度风险：
面试暴露点：
投递风险：
面试前1天必须补：
面试前3天尽量补：
长期补强：
替代方向/补强路径：
不能包装成事实的内容：
```

If the fit level is **不建议投递**, use a risk-first tone: explain the low-fit reasons, do not make the resume look high-fit, and provide safer wording, alternative target roles, or a gap-closing path. The user decides whether to apply.

Be direct, but do not discourage the user. The goal is preparation, not judgment.
