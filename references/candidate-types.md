# Candidate Types

Use this reference to choose interview questions and resume emphasis.

## 社招

Required inputs:

- Target JD and platform.
- Company, title, dates, reporting/collaboration context.
- 2-4 strongest projects or responsibilities.
- Metrics: revenue, conversion, traffic, cost, efficiency, retention, delivery quality, or team scale.
- Tools, methods, systems, and industry context.

Ask:

- "这段经历里你真正负责的边界是什么？谁给目标，谁验收结果？"
- "有没有可确认的前后对比数字？没有数字时，有没有范围、排名、交付物或复盘证据？"
- "如果面试官追问这个结果，你能讲清楚口径和你的个人贡献吗？"

Default structure:

1. Personal profile / 个人优势
2. Work experience
3. Key projects
4. Skills
5. Education

## 应届生

Do not require work-like projects. Mine proof of learning and execution.
If the user only provides labels such as school, major, tools, or target role, use `from-scratch-intake.md` and do not draft a resume yet.

Useful evidence:

- Internship, graduation thesis/design, coursework project, competition, club operation, part-time job, volunteer work.
- Self-built demo, analysis report, content account, portfolio, GitHub/Gitee, design portfolio, research notes.
- Grades, ranking, scholarships, certificates only when they strengthen the target role.

Ask:

- "你最接近目标岗位的一次实践是什么？哪怕是课程作业或模拟项目也可以。"
- "有没有具体产出物：报告、代码、作品集、活动结果、数据看板、文章链接？"
- "如果没有相关项目，面试前能不能补一个3天内可完成的小作品？"

Default structure:

1. Job objective and profile
2. Education
3. Internship / practice / project
4. Campus / competition / portfolio
5. Skills and certificates

## 实习

Emphasize potential and reliability.
If concrete practice is missing, use `from-scratch-intake.md` to mine coursework, mini projects, campus work, and availability evidence before drafting.

Required inputs:

- Availability date, weekly days, internship duration.
- Relevant courses, tools, mini projects, campus work.
- Evidence of fast learning and careful execution.

Avoid senior wording such as `主导业务增长` unless true.

## 转行

Translate prior experience into target-role evidence.
When the old resume is hollow or far from the target role, collect concrete source-role actions first, then map only defensible transferable evidence.

Ask:

- "原行业里哪些工作和目标岗位同构？例如用户沟通、数据分析、项目推进、销售转化、内容生产。"
- "你为转行补过什么：课程、证书、作品、实习、免费项目、案例拆解？"
- "面试官最可能质疑你哪一点？"

Default output must include a short transition explanation and a gap-closing plan.

## 高管或专家

Emphasize scale and judgment.

Ask:

- Team size, budget, revenue/cost scope, decision ownership.
- Business stage, strategy tradeoffs, organization design, crisis examples.
- Industry relationships and public proof where appropriate.

Avoid over-dense resumes; include a hunter-forwardable executive summary.
