# Resume Strategy

Use this before evidence audit. The goal is to decide what the resume should prove before deciding what to ask or write.

## Good Resume Standard

A strong domestic China resume should:

- Show the target positioning in the first 30 seconds.
- Put the strongest matching evidence early.
- Make each core experience prove a capability, not just list tasks.
- State personal boundary, method/tool, deliverable, and result when the claim is important.
- Use conservative wording for evidence that is real but incomplete.
- Keep resume, platform scripts, interview answers, and risk notes on the same fact source.

Avoid:

- Equal-weight dumping of every experience.
- Generic self-evaluation without role signal.
- Turning labels like `会SQL`, `负责运营`, or `参与项目` into strong bullets.
- Writing a high-fit resume when the evidence only supports conservative or low-fit delivery.

## Strategy Decision

Before writing, decide:

```text
目标岗位筛选重点：
硬门槛：
Top 3核心能力：
候选人定位：强匹配 / 相近转化 / 保守投递 / 不建议投递
主线卖点：
次级卖点：
需要弱化或删除：
预期结构：
交付模式：正式投递版 / 保守投递版 / 草稿校对版 / 采集清单 / 不建议投递包
```

Do not generate a formal resume until this decision exists.

## Candidate Positioning

- **强匹配**: hard gates pass and top 3 capabilities have direct defensible evidence.
- **相近转化**: at least one core capability is direct, and the rest have clear transferable actions.
- **保守投递**: facts exist but evidence is incomplete, indirect, or hard to quantify.
- **不建议投递**: hard gates fail or the role's core work has no defensible evidence.

## Material States

- **Sufficient**: enough facts to write the main selling line and core bullets. Proceed to evidence audit and final claim review.
- **Messy**: many facts but no clear priority. Cluster by target capability, then choose a main line.
- **Hollow**: responsibilities or tools without scene, action, output, or result. Ask targeted probes before writing.
- **Sparse**: only labels, school, skills, certificates, or target role. Output intake checklist or gap-closing path.

## Resume Shape

Default section order:

```text
个人优势
工作经历
项目经历
教育经历
技能证书
```

Adjust only when it improves screening:

- Campus/internship: education and projects may move earlier.
- Career transition: add a short positioning summary and emphasize transferable evidence.
- Senior/expert: leadership scale, business impact, and decision quality move earlier.

## Project Selection

Choose projects that prove the strategy. For each core project, answer:

```text
This project proves:
The strongest defensible claim is:
The claim cannot exceed:
```

If the answer is unclear, merge, downgrade, or omit the project.
