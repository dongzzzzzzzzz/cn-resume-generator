# Claim-Led Probing

Use this after the first intake or deep-probe pass. The goal is not to ask more questions; it is to verify what the resume is allowed to claim.

## Core Rule

Probe from the intended resume claim backward:

```text
卖点假设：
想写成的claim：
必须证明的信息：
已知证据：
缺口：
追问目的：
允许写法：
降级写法：
禁止写法：
```

Every follow-up question must explain which claim or writing strength it verifies.

## Probe Budget

- Ask at most 3 questions per round.
- Ask by resume or core-experience group, not by every possible claim.
- Use at most 2 rounds before choosing formal, conservative, draft/checking, intake checklist, or risk-first delivery.
- If a claim only affects wording strength, use conservative wording instead of extending the interview.

## Claim Types

### Ownership

Use for words such as `独立`, `主导`, `负责核心`, `搭建`, `设计`, `统筹`.

Verify:

- What exactly did the candidate personally decide, build, execute, or maintain?
- What was done by teammates, vendors, managers, or existing systems?
- Was the work independently owned, jointly delivered, or only participated in?

Allowed outcomes:

- Strong: `独立负责/主导` only when personal boundary and decisions are clear.
- Conservative: `负责其中X部分`, `参与设计并落地`, `配合完成`.
- Forbidden: upgrading participation into ownership.

### Metric or Result

Use for percentages, before/after results, revenue, conversion, cycle time, efficiency, accuracy, cost, quality, defect, satisfaction, or volume.

Verify:

- What is the denominator and time period?
- How was the metric measured?
- Which part was caused by the candidate's work, and which part came from team/product/market changes?
- Is it a final business result, process metric, or internal estimate?

Allowed outcomes:

- Strong: precise metric with clear basis and contribution boundary.
- Conservative: range, approximate wording, or process result.
- Forbidden: claiming full causality for team or external outcomes.

### Scope and Scale

Use for words such as `全流程`, `全部`, `平台`, `体系`, `团队级`, `集团`, `多部门`, `核心业务`.

Verify:

- Who used it and how often?
- Which processes, teams, customers, stores, accounts, systems, or regions are included?
- What is excluded?
- Was it adopted, piloted, or only personally used?

Allowed outcomes:

- Strong: scope is confirmed and repeatable.
- Conservative: `用于个人/小组/试点场景`, `覆盖部分核心流程`.
- Forbidden: writing a pilot, personal tool, or partial work as a platform or full-process system.

### Method or Technical/Professional Depth

Use for claims about architecture, strategy, evaluation, analysis, design, compliance, process improvement, diagnosis, or expert methods.

Verify:

- What problem did the method solve?
- What alternatives were considered?
- What key tradeoff or decision did the candidate make?
- What failure mode, exception, or risk was handled?

Allowed outcomes:

- Strong: method choice, decision logic, and result are clear.
- Conservative: `使用X方法完成Y`, without overstating originality.
- Forbidden: turning tool usage into architecture, strategy, or expert-level design.

## Answer Digestion

After each user answer, update the audit before generating:

```text
可写内容：
需保守表达：
仍需确认：
终稿禁止/降级清单：
下一步模式：
```

If the user answer is vague, do not invent missing context. Either ask one more targeted question within the budget or use a weaker version.

## Project Bullet Shape

Use `3+1`:

1. Background/goal: why the work mattered.
2. Solution/key action: what the candidate did and how.
3. Result/impact: what changed and how it is measured.
4. Optional: challenge, tradeoff, boundary, or reusable method.

Do not force the optional bullet when the evidence is thin.

## Generic Examples

- For a technical tool project, verify pain point, input/output, flow, failure handling, adoption scope, metric basis, and personal boundary before writing `system`, `platform`, or `independently built`.
- For a sales case, verify lead source, customer need, decision chain, candidate action, team support, deal size, and payback before writing `closed`, `key account`, or revenue impact.
- For an operations case, verify target group, strategy, funnel step, candidate-owned action, data source, and attribution before writing conversion or growth results.
- For a functional/process case, verify process boundary, risk handled, approval or compliance limits, cycle/cost/error baseline, and stakeholder feedback before writing efficiency or control impact.
